I'm Patrick, based in the UK. I make things around games: mods, small tools and the websites that grow up beside them. Most of it starts with a question about how something works, or with a game missing a feature I wanted. That curiosity carries over into rendering and shaders, web design and the photographs I take in games.

## Selected work

**[s&box Explorer](https://sbox.grimtech.co.uk/)**<br>A discovery site for s&box: browse packages and creators, follow player activity and see what the community is building.

**[76Radio](https://github.com/PatrickJnr/76Radio)**<br>A Fallout 76 mod that brings Spotify controls and internet radio into the Pip-Boy's RADIO tab.

**[Virtual Photography](https://vp.grimtech.co.uk/)**<br>In-game photography from the worlds I spend time in, focused on composition, atmosphere and the details that are easy to walk past.

### More projects

- **[WorkshopCollections](https://github.com/PatrickJnr/WorkshopCollections)** adds subscribed Steam Workshop items to a collection without clicking through them one by one.
- **[Shader Cache Remover](https://github.com/PatrickJnr/Shader-Cache-Remover)** finds and safely clears stale shader caches to free up disk space and resolve graphical issues.

## Tools and interests

Games are where most of my technical curiosity goes. I work with **C# and Unity**, write **C++** for mods, and spend time with **Source 2 and s&box** when I want to see how an engine works. Rendering and shaders are usually the parts I keep reading about long after the original problem is solved.

On the web I use **PHP, JavaScript, HTML and CSS**, usually with **SQLite** or **PostgreSQL** underneath. **Python** handles the utilities and automation around a project. Steam integrations keep finding their way into the mix.

More experiments and older projects live in [my repositories](https://github.com/PatrickJnr?tab=repositories).
